How to play: 100 incoming papers, only 15 headed for your four desks, and just 20 stamps. Watch their wiggly observed trails and decide which papers are actually going to hit a desk. Tap or click a paper to stamp it. Most will miss harmlessly. Waiting gives you more observations, but less time to respond. No flashes, strobe effects, or loud sounds.

Scoring: stop real threat +100 · let harmless paper pass +25 · stamp harmless paper −50 · desk hit −200 · unused stamp at end +10. A stamp only hits the paper you click close enough to; missed clicks still use a stamp. Desks can be damaged once each.
